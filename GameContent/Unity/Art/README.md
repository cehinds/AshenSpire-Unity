# Campaign enemy artwork

EnemyAtlas.png was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. Original upstream assets were preserved. `tools/import-enemy-atlas.py` extracts the twelve connected sprite groups, applies one shared scale and aligns feet at (192,364) in 384x384 transparent frames. EnemyAtlas.json records the source SHA-256, source boxes and scale. Campaign Foes refer to the resulting `painted_*` Resources/Art assets.

Generation prompt:

Create a production-ready 2D game enemy sprite atlas for AshenSpire, a dark painterly fantasy card roguelike. EXACT 4 columns by 3 rows, twelve equally sized cells, all characters full body, individually centered, feet at the same 90 percent cell baseline, generous transparent padding between cells, every silhouette contained entirely within its own cell. Transparent RGBA background with genuinely transparent empty areas; no scenery, shadows outside feet, labels, borders, text, checkerboard or grid lines. Consistent hand-painted game-sprite style: worn charcoal metal, muted bone and forest colors, restrained warm gold and ember edge lights, strongly readable silhouettes on a small phone. All face three-quarter LEFT toward the player, no photorealism, no cute cartoon. Row 1 left to right: Wandering Soldier battered helmet sword and shield; Blight Hound lean diseased quadruped with muted green lesions; Living Armor hollow heavily plated knight with shield; Fell Warden imposing ash knight with ember halberd. Row 2: Court Duelist lean masked noble swordsman; Court Surgeon sinister robed plague healer holding a bone tool; Gilded Knight heavy tarnished golden plate and greatsword; Stitched King towering stitched flesh tyrant with crown and heavy cleaver. Row 3: Grave Wisp floating ragged skeletal spirit teal glow; Wyrm Aspirant hunched reptilian warrior with curved blade; Valkyrie Shade spectral winged female warrior with spear; Wyrm Lord imposing horned dragon humanoid with folded wings and ember chest. Keep each sprite large in cell but safely separated; use all available detail. One coherent sheet, landscape aspect ratio 4:3.

Some weapons crossed nominal grid boundaries in the generated sheet. Extraction therefore groups connected transparent silhouettes rather than cutting fixed cells, preserving the visible weapons. Inspect the source and manifest when replacing this atlas.
